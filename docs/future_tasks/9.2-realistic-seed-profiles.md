# 9.2 Realistic demo / onboarding seed profiles

- **Section:** 9. Commercial readiness
- **Priority:** 🟡 medium — **Feature 2 (hold; do NOT spec yet)**
- **Track:** B (product) — research-gated
- **Depends on:** §9.1 (same generator) + the finance-habits / budget-app research

- **What:** A realism layer on top of the §9.1 generator that produces
  **believable household profiles** — plausible category mixes, amounts, and
  cadences — for demo, first-run empty-state, marketing screenshots, and design
  validation.
- **How it works:** research-informed distributions layered over the coverage
  engine from §9.1; the engine is shared, only the "what's realistic" inputs differ.
- **Ortho fit:** solves the empty-state problem a commercial launch faces and lets
  us judge whether the dashboard/insights *feel* right on real distributions.
- **Why held:** its inputs (what real households actually look like) don't exist
  yet. Speccing it before the research means writing throwaway realism — promote
  it only after the research lands.

> Source: the commercial-readiness & sequencing review (2026-07-17).
