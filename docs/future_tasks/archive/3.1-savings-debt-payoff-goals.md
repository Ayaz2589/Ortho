# 3.1 Savings / debt-payoff goals

> **✅ Delivered — spec 027 (`specs/027-savings-goals/`), 2026-07-18.** `goals` +
> `goal_contributions` tables (member RLS), the calm progress view
> (`web/components/goals/`, reached from Settings → Goals), the pure vectored
> engine `web/lib/finance/goals.ts` (`goals.json`), and the one "goal off-track"
> insight rule. Progress is **contribution-driven** (bank balances aren't synced —
> spec 024 is connect-only); the linked account/category is context only. The
> "monthly auto-contribution nudge" is surfaced as the suggested amount *inside*
> the off-track insight. Balance-driven progress and auto-contribution-from-category
> remain future work.

- **Section:** 3. Planning & goals
- **Priority:** 🟡 medium

- **What:** Named goals ("emergency fund", "trip") with a target + progress,
  linked to cash flow.
- **How it works:** a `goals` table (name, target cents, target date, linked
  account/category); progress = contributions or an account's balance vs target;
  optional monthly auto-contribution nudge in insights.
- **Ortho fit:** clean, self-contained, privacy-safe. Fits our cents model and
  the insights engine (a "goal off-track" rule). Good early candidate.
