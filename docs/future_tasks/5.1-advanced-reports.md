# 5.1 Advanced reports (Sankey cash-flow, savings rate, custom charts)

- **Section:** 5. Intelligence & reporting
- **Priority:** ⚪ low / exploratory

- **What:** Customizable charts — income→spend Sankey, savings-rate over time,
  category/merchant deep-dives.
- **How it works:** aggregate transactions into user-selected dimensions/date
  ranges; render configurable chart types.
- **Ortho fit:** we have fixed calm dashboard widgets; this adds a "reports"
  surface. Keep it calm (no chart-junk). Reuses our aggregate RPCs
  (`lib/api/aggregates.ts`, wired by the spec 027 slice below).

- **Status:** a **first slice shipped in spec 027**
  (`specs/027-reports-mvp/`): a segmented `Overview | Reports` mode inside the
  existing Dashboard page (not a new route/destination) with exactly two views —
  **savings-rate over time** (`web/lib/reports/savings.ts`) and **category
  deep-dive** (`web/lib/reports/categories.ts` + `months.ts`), lazy-loaded and
  wired to the previously-unwired aggregate RPC wrappers
  (`web/lib/api/aggregates.ts`). **Still open from this §5.1 item:** the
  income→spend Sankey, custom/configurable charts, and personal (non-household)
  transactions in reports.
