# 9.4 Finance-model correctness & honest labels

- **Section:** 9. Commercial readiness
- **Priority:** 🔴 high (verifications) · 🟡 medium (labels/policy)
- **Track:** A (correctness) + small B (labeling)
- **Verifies against:** the §9.1 coverage corpus

A grouped set of correctness/trust items surfaced in the readiness review. Two are
**unverified findings** to confirm first; the rest are confirmed.

- **A2 — Timezone insight-bucketing** 🔴 *(verify → fix).* `insights.ts` builds
  *local* month windows but compares against `new Date(t.date)` parsed as *UTC*
  midnight, so a boundary-dated row can land in the wrong month for users west of
  UTC. Masked for app-created rows (stored noon-UTC), exposed for imported/legacy/
  iOS rows. Invisible to CI, which pins `TZ=UTC`. Reproduce via §9.1 under a
  non-UTC TZ, align the regimes, add the missing non-UTC test.
- **A4 — CLI leftover-cent divergence** 🟡 *(verify → fix).* The import CLI orders
  owners by `sort_order` while the app/iOS canonicalize by lexical id
  (`orderedOwnerIds`); when they disagree, the leftover cent lands on a different
  person for imported vs app-created splits. Confirm with a `sort_order` ≠ id
  household, then unify on the canonical order.
- **A3 — Extend the independent oracle** 🟡. The spec-025 oracle asserts hand-
  derived truth for ~6 simple engines but skips the riskiest: `insights.ts`, the
  float-dollar amortization schedule, filters, lease timing. Add independent
  goldens/invariants so a regression can't be laundered into the vectors.
- **B3 — Honest labels for approximations** 🟡. "Equity" ignores market value
  (really *principal paid down*); "Net rental" subtracts only P&I (no taxes /
  insurance / maintenance); a paid-off mortgage perpetually shows ~$2 of debt.
  Relabel (cheap) or model properly (better).
- **B4 — Rounding-cent fairness policy** ⚪. The leftover cent always goes to the
  canonically-first owner (not largest-remainder). Sub-cent and deterministic, but
  make it a **conscious, documented** choice before strangers split bills on it.

> Source: the commercial-readiness & sequencing review (2026-07-17). A2 and A4 are
> agent-surfaced and **not yet independently verified** — verify before fixing.
